The Play

The BRRRR Strategy

The proven way to build a real-estate portfolio without needing a fortune to start. Five moves, run with patience and honest numbers.

Buy
Move 1
Find the deal

Buy right — undervalued or distressed property others walk past. You make your money the day you buy.

Rehab
Move 2
Fix it up

Rehab it honest and solid — safe, clean, and worth more. Sweat equity turns a wreck into a home.

Rent
Move 3
Rent it right

Put in a good tenant at a fair rent. Now the property pays you every month instead of costing you.

Refinance
Move 4
Pull your cash back

Refinance on the new, higher value — recover most of what you put in, tax-advantaged, while you keep the asset.

Repeat
Move 5
Do it again

Take that same cash and buy the next one. That's how one house quietly becomes a street.

Where It Started

Our first deed

206 Ellington St · New Bern, NC🏠 Property No. 1

Our first home on the board was a little flood-damaged house most folks had written off. We bought it right, rehabbed it with our own hands, rented it, and worked it until it was paid off free and clear.

No fortune to start with — just the willingness to do the work and play the long game. That one house is proof the strategy works, and the reason we keep buying.

We keep our numbers honest and our stories real — no inflated returns, no get-rich-quick promises. Real estate takes patience, capital, and risk, and not every deal wins. This is what worked for us; results vary.

Where the Blueprint Came From

The books that rebuilt me

Before I ever rebuilt a house, I had to rebuild my mind.

My money mind was born in my hardest chapter — years I'm not proud of, but that God redeemed. A few books came from Tiffany, who mailed them in. Most came through a free prison book program a brother pointed me to. I read them over and over, out on the track. Rich Dad Poor Dad flipped how I saw money — assets versus liabilities — and the classics that followed taught me cash flow, patience, and the long game. The seeds of every property on this page.

But it wasn't just the books — it was the brothers. We walked circles on that track all day — Brad, Wayne, Josh, Blake, and Crazy Man — knocked out push-ups and pull-ups, played the radio, talked faith, and prayed. Iron sharpening iron. Some of the best men I've ever known, doing the hard, quiet work of becoming better.

And every Sunday, Living Proof Ministry (Robbie & Gale) walked through those doors and never gave up on us. 🙏

Second chances are real — for people and for property.

If a house everyone wrote off can be rebuilt, so can a life. That's the whole reason I do this. To God be the glory. — See more of the books that changed me →
